Introducing Libertad Value Track

An interactive research platform designed to help collectors examine historical reported eBay sold prices, monthly market trends, and price distributions across different years, finishes, grading companies, and grades.

Our mission is simple: make meaningful market information accessible to collectors, without gatekeeping or cherry-picked transactions.
